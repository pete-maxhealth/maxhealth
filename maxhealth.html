A 50g daily carb ceiling is set. This reflects the very low carb approach used to exploit GBM's glucose dependency — the same protocol documented in the GBM Patient Protocol Guide →

The ketosis zone indicator on your dashboard will highlight when your carb intake is within the therapeutic range. You can adjust the ceiling at any time in Settings.

Sync Wearable Data
One command syncs everything — moves exports from Downloads, runs the pipeline, writes combined.csv.
bash /storage/emulated/0/maxhealth/app/maxhealth/sync.sh
Open Termux, paste and run. When it's done, tap Load combined.csv below.
For Amazfit/Zepp: extract the zip with ZArchiver first, then run sync.sh.
Load combined.csv into History & Trends

After sync.sh completes, load combined.csv to populate History and Trends. Existing manually-logged entries are preserved.

cd /storage/emulated/0/maxhealth/app
