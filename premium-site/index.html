Your health data, syncing itself.

The free web version of MaxedHealth handles logging, nutrition tracking, imports, reports and manual entry. Premium is the version that runs on your own Android phone and does the rest for you, even with no signal.

Automatic wearable sync

Steps, heart rate, sleep and more flow in through Health Connect in the background. No exports, no copying files.

Offline mode that really works

Opens with no signal at all. Log meals, review your data, build charts and PDF reports, and have answers read aloud, all without a connection. Meals you save for later go through when you are back online, and the app never freezes waiting on a dead connection.

Extractors for your devices

Withings, RingConn, Zepp / Amazfit, Garmin and Health Connect files are read for you. Own a tracker that is not supported yet? Premium lets a new extractor be added to the pipeline, so your device is not left out.

You choose which device wins

Wearing two devices? Rank them for each measure, such as sleep or steps, and Premium merges the data your way. Conflicting nights are flagged for you to settle.

Your data stays on your phone

Everything is stored on your own device and included in your backups, not in a browser that can be cleared.

Looks after itself

Restarts after a reboot, updates itself, and has a built-in health check. If background sync ever stops, the app tells you in plain words.

Setup help and updates

Guided setup, ongoing updates and someone to ask when something is not right.
