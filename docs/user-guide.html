01 — Core loop

Your daily
workflow.

Four steps. Once it's habit, each takes under a minute.

01
Morning
Log your weight
On the Today tab, tap UPDATE. Weigh yourself first thing — naked, after the bathroom, before eating. Same conditions every day makes the trend meaningful.
Why it matters: daily weight fluctuates 1–2kg from food, water, and digestion. One number means nothing. Thirty numbers tell the real story.
02
Throughout the day
Log meals as you eat them
Tap the Nutrition tab. There are four ways to log food — use whichever fits the moment:

📝 Type it — describe what you ate in plain English. The AI identifies components and calculates macros automatically.
2 scrambled eggs with butter and smoked salmon chicken thigh 180g with roasted broccoli baked beans on toast
▦ Scan a barcode — tap the barcode button, point at any product. Looks up exact values from Open Food Facts (3M+ products). Enter the amount and log.

🔍 Search the database — tap the search button, type a product or brand name. Select from results, enter grams, done.

🏷 Label photo — tap the camera, switch to Label mode, photograph a nutrition label, type the product name and amount. AI reads the exact per-100g values and scales to your amount.

📷 Meal photo — photograph a plate. AI estimates components and portions. Tap "✏ Correct this" if anything is wrong.

Library: save any food to your Library for instant one-tap future logging. Recipes save multi-ingredient meals — set servings once, log one portion with a tap.
03
Optional
Close out for an instant summary (not required)
The day saves to History and master.csv automatically at midnight regardless — this step is a convenience, not a requirement. If you'd like an immediate summary and server sync rather than waiting, type any of these in the Log tab when you're done eating:
that's me done · done for today · all done · wrapping up
MaxedHealth shows your daily summary right away and pushes the row to master.csv on your server immediately, instead of waiting for the automatic midnight rollover. It also stamps today's weigh-in onto the History entry. Skip it entirely if you'd rather — nothing is lost either way.
04
Throughout the day
Track your water
On the Today tab, scroll to the 💧 Water card. Tap + Glass (250ml), + Can (330ml), + Bottle (500ml), or + Custom. The progress bar fills toward your daily target (default 2000ml). When you hit your target it flashes blue.
Water history appears in the History tab and Trends chart after midnight rollover — the same as wearable data.
Fibre: the 🌾 Fibre card on Today shows today's fibre against your target (30g by default, the UK guideline for adults; change it in Settings) and your 14-day average. Only foods that carry a fibre figure count, and a day with none is left out rather than counted as zero. Check which foods are missing fibre and other figures lists your library's gaps: type the fibre from the label, or tap No fibre for foods that genuinely have none. Nothing is ever filled in for you.
05
Optional
Set your day mode
Tell the app what kind of day it is before you start logging:

🟢 Standard — full carb ceiling applies
🟡 Occasion — social event, slightly more flexibility
🔴 Holiday — ceiling not enforced

The ketosis badge and AI feedback adjust automatically.
🔍 Can't find something? The search icon in the header searches everything at once — Library, Recipes, Saved Prompts, and every Settings section. Tapping a result takes you straight there, no need to remember which tab something lives in.
02 — Data files

Two files.
Both matter.

MaxedHealth uses two CSV files for different purposes.

master.csv
Your nutrition history
Your nutrition history — one row per day, written automatically at close-out:
DD/MM/YY|Calories|Protein|Carbs|Notes 09/05/26|2840|173|40|chicken dinner

Auto-saved: close-out writes directly to master.csv — no copying needed.

Missed a day? Go to History tab → + Missed day. Describe what you ate and the AI calculates the totals, or enter them manually.

Backup: automatic daily to your local server (Local mode) — see Backing up ↓ for the full picture, or Settings → Import → Full App State for an immediate one any time.
combined.csv
Your wearable data
Wearable data, one row per day, merged from your devices — steps, sleep, HR, HRV, SpO2, weight.

Source: Premium creates it automatically; in the free web app you can import a file like it.

Import: loads automatically after Sync Now. To load one manually, tick Or sync manually under the Sync Now button on the Import tab to reveal the manual file picker.

What it unlocks: the Trends tab — sleep, activity, HR, weight and body composition (muscle mass, fat, bone, hydration) plotted over time alongside your nutrition. Tap any metric card for a full drill-down chart.
03 — Nutrition tools

Every way to
log food.

Seven methods. Use whichever fits — they all feed the same daily total.

🎤
Voice
Tap the mic and just talk. Voice works two different ways depending on where you are in the app:
Continuous (meal logging, missed-day, library batch-add, recipes, supplements) — the mic keeps listening while you say several things one after another. Say each item, then say "done" — or plain English like "I've finished now", "that's everything", or "stop" — to wrap up. Tapping the mic mid-flow pauses rather than cancels; tap again to pick up exactly where you left off.
Single-shot (weight, water) — one value, said once. No "done" phrase needed, since there's only ever one thing to say.
▦
Barcode scanner
Tap the barcode button in the Log tab's input bar. Point your camera at any product barcode. MaxedHealth looks it up on Open Food Facts — exact product name, calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugar, salt, iron. Enter the amount in grams and log. Works on almost any packaged food in the UK and worldwide.
🔍
Food database search
Tap the search button. Type a product name, brand or ingredient. Results appear automatically — tap one to select it, enter how many grams you had, and log. Same database as the barcode scanner — 3M+ products.
🏷
Label photo
Take a photo of a nutrition label. Switch to Label mode (it auto-selects when a photo is attached). Type the product name and amount in the text box — e.g. "ASDA Cottage Cheese 150g". The AI reads the exact per-100g values from the label and scales to your stated amount.
Label mode reads the left column on UK labels — always per 100g, never per serving.
📷
Meal photo
Take a photo of your plate. The AI identifies the components and estimates portions. If anything's wrong, tap ✏ Correct this beneath the logged entry — describe the correction and it re-logs automatically.
📚
Library
Save any food to your Library for instant one-tap future logging. Values are confirmed and locked — no AI guessing on repeat items. Tap EDIT to change values or delete. Tap LOG to log instantly. Or build several new library entries in one go with 🎤 Voice add — say a food and its amount, say the next, say "done" when you're through.
Every item auto-sorts into real food categories (meat, vegetable, fat, grain, sweet, and so on) — Browse by Category lets you see and log from a whole group at once. Add your own category to anything via the item's edit screen if the auto-guess isn't quite right.
Editing library values only affects future logging — not meals already recorded. To correct a past entry, go to the History tab.
🍳
Recipes
Save multi-ingredient meals as recipes in the Library tab. Set the number of servings (e.g. 4 for a batch curry). Next time, tap Log 1 serving — you'll see a review screen with every ingredient listed first, where you can add, remove, or swap anything before it actually hits your log. Add ingredients from your Library, the food database, barcode, or manually.
🎤 Genuinely useful hands-free while actually cooking — prop the phone somewhere you can hear it and just talk through each ingredient as you add it to the pan: "200 grams chicken thigh... one tablespoon olive oil... two cloves garlic...". No touching a greasy or floury screen mid-recipe. Say "done" once you're through, then review and save as normal.
🔄 Any ingredient — in a recipe, Cook Mode, or a suggested meal combo — can be swapped for something else via the substitute button. Opens a real searchable picker across your library (with an online-search fallback if nothing fits) rather than guessing at a match automatically — the portion size carries over, only the ingredient itself changes.
If you've corrected an ingredient's values in the Library since building a recipe with it, tap Refresh from Library on the recipe to pull the update through — it won't happen on its own.
🥗
Meal suggestions from your own library
Tap Next meal idea to get a real suggestion built entirely from what's actually in your library and today's remaining macros — never invented. Tap the categories you want (meat, vegetable, grain, and so on), adjust the number next to any if you want more than one, then Send.
Works by typing too — "protein and pasta" or "meat and 2 veg" straight into the chat box does the same thing without opening the picker.
If your library genuinely can't cover what you've asked for, you'll get a real explanation of what's missing rather than a suggestion quietly padded out with the wrong thing.
⚖️
Balance my macros
The deliberate opposite of Next meal idea: that one starts from what you fancy (pick categories yourself), this one starts from what today still needs — no picker, just tap it. Uses the exact same remaining-budget and library-only engine, so suggestions are always real, loggable items.
If there's genuinely too little left in the day, or it's getting late, it says so plainly first — then offers a few smaller, snack-sized ideas instead of quietly padding out a "meal" that doesn't really fit.
04 — Corrections

Made a
mistake?

Every entry is editable. Nothing is permanent until you decide it is.

✏
Edit today's entries
On the Today tab, tap any entry in Today's Log. For a single-item entry, an inline editor opens — change the description, calories, protein or carbs. For a multi-ingredient entry, it opens a full per-ingredient editor instead: adjust any single ingredient's amount (its macros auto-scale with it), add or remove ingredients, or use Scale entire entry to X% to adjust every ingredient at once — useful if you only ate part of a bigger batch. Tap Save. The daily total updates instantly. Tap × to remove an entry entirely — you have 5 seconds to undo.
📅
Edit previous days
Go to the History tab, expand any day, and tap any entry to edit it — the same per-ingredient editor described above works here too for multi-ingredient entries. The day totals recalculate automatically. Tap ✏ Edit day totals to override the whole day's figures without editing individual entries.
➕
Log a missed day
Go to the History tab and tap + Missed day. Enter the date, then either describe what you ate (AI calculates the totals) or enter the numbers directly. Saves to History and master.csv automatically.
05 — Wearables

Wearable
data.

Sleep, heart rate, HRV, steps and weight make your trends far more useful. There are two ways to get them in.

✍️
Free: enter or import
Type values on the Manual Entry screen, or import a CSV file from the Import tab. Both work in the free web app.
🛡️
Glitches are caught
If a device file has an impossible value (a 5,000 kg weight, a negative night of sleep, a heart rate of 300), that one value is left out before it reaches History, so it cannot skew a chart or a statistic. The rest of that row still comes in.
⌚
Premium: automatic
Premium reads Health Connect and your ring or watch for you every day, picks the most trustworthy device for each measure, and checks its own work. Nothing to type.
06 — Analysis

Reports
& Trends.

Available once you have data. The longer you track, the more useful these become.

📈
Trends
Weight, sleep, HR, HRV, SpO2, steps — over time alongside nutrition. Needs wearable data (Premium sync, or a file you import). Each chart's style row has a colour swatch to recolour its main line (double-tap to go back to the default).
📋
Summary cards
Average macros, adherence %, streak, and best day — for any date range you choose.
🔍
Query builder
"Days under protein target?" · "Days over carb ceiling?" · "Current streak?" Answered instantly. Save any query with a name to re-run later with fresh dates.
💬
Ask AI & Saved Prompts
Ask any open-ended question about your data in plain English — sleep patterns, HRV correlations, "how am I doing today". If you've genuinely changed condition at some point (Settings → Condition History tracks this automatically), just ask naturally to compare periods — "how does this period compare to before" — no filter needed, the AI works it out from the real dates. Every question can be saved with a name for one-tap reuse later, searchable and editable in one shared library across both the Log-tab chat and this Ask AI panel. Dictate new ones by voice; the list sorts by how often you actually use each one.
🔊 Any AI answer — here, a saved prompt's reply, a Full Summary or Monthly Summary report — has a read-aloud button. It splits longer answers into natural chunks behind the scenes rather than reading the whole thing as one block, so a full multi-paragraph report reads properly start to finish instead of stalling partway through.
🤖
AI Brief
Plain-English narrative of your nutrition period. GBM patients get metabolic adherence analysis.
🧭
Topic chips
Trends, Reports and the Settings screens have a row of chips at the top (for example All · Body · Heart & sleep · Food · Patterns). Tap one to show only that topic. It only changes what you see: nothing is moved, deleted or un-hidden, and All looks as it always did.
⚖️
Weight unit
Settings → Profile → Weight unit: kg, lbs or st + lb. Switching never changes your data, only how it is shown. In st + lb you can type "14 st 2" in one box. Charts follow the unit too.
📅
Add or fix a day's weight
Missed a weigh-in? The Update Weight box has a Date field, and you can also use "＋ Add or fix a day's weight" on the Trends weight card or weight drill-down, tap a gap on Your Journey, or use the Weight box in History → Edit day totals.
〰️
Gaps and the faint "last known" line
On Your Journey and the Trends weight chart, a faint dotted line carries your last real weight across days with no weigh-in so a gap reads as "no new data". It is only drawn, never saved, and does not affect averages. On Your Journey a break of more than a week shows as "⋯ 18d".
🗺️
Your Journey overlays
Chips above the Your Journey chart add Calories, Protein, Carbs, Water, Logged exercise, Active calories or Steps as dashed lines, each scaled to its own maximum. Tap a day to see the real figures. Your choice is remembered.
🔬
Body Composition card
Weight alone can hide what changed. This Trends card compares this week's smart-scale readings with last week's and says in plain words whether you are, for example, losing fat and gaining muscle. Scale estimates can swing by half a kilo or more, so treat it as a guide.
🆚
Compare Two Periods
On Trends (Patterns chip). Pick a recent stretch, such as a holiday where meals were photographed, and a baseline before it. The card compares logged calories, steps and weight trend, and tells you whether your logging looks too high or too low. It always shows a margin of error and says when the difference is just noise. "What was counted" lists exactly which days were used. Short periods give wide margins; extra weigh-ins help.
🏋️
Copy previous set
In the routine editor and the workout Apply screen, tap ⧉ on a set (or "⧉ copy previous set" on an exercise) to repeat its reps, weight and note, so 3 x 10 @ 40 kg is one line plus taps.
🍽️
Tableware & portion sizes
Settings → Tableware & Portion Sizes lists your plate, bowl, mug, glass and spoon sizes, with typical sizes filled in. Add sets for other places (holiday, parents' house). Photo estimates and the 3-AI check use them. The ↺ button returns a value to its default.
🌦️
Local weather
Optional card on Today. Type a town or use your location (only rounded coordinates go to the free Open-Meteo service). It raises the water target on hot days, suggests the day's exercise effort, and shows the best time to exercise outdoors. A location set with "Use my location" follows you: Refresh, and opening the app, re-read your position and move the weather if you have travelled more than a few kilometres. A town you typed stays put. You can always override it.
✈️
Working offline
With no connection the app still opens, and logging, your library, history and charts keep working. A banner shows at the top. AI features say "needs internet". A meal the app can't recognise can be saved for later; when you are back online tap Review and confirm each one, which logs it on the day you saved it.
06b — Move, sleep & statistics

Exercise, sleep
& your own numbers.

Everything you log about movement and sleep feeds the same places as your food: History, the chart, Reports, summaries, the AI and your backup.

🏃
Add exercise from a list
On Today, the Activity card has ＋ Add exercise. Pick from 55+ known exercises, grouped by kind (walk and hike, running, cycling, water, racket and court, gym, classes and more) and tagged indoor or outdoor, or add one free-text line of your own. Known exercises carry their own energy values, so you are not guessing a number.
🧮
How the calories are worked out
Calories = MET × your weight × hours. A walk, run or ride with a distance and a time uses your real speed (values from the 2011 Compendium of Physical Activities, level ground); anything else uses the Easy / Moderate / Hard level, and choosing a level by hand always wins. Exercise is counted net of resting energy (MET minus 1) because your daily target already includes what you burn sitting still. Once your watch has compared enough days for a kind of exercise, its measured ratio replaces the formula for that kind. Settings → Formulas has a Net / Gross switch. These are estimates, not measurements.
🎯
Plan ahead and the In / Out strip
The Activity card's 🎯 PLAN AHEAD shows what a walk, a session or extra steps would earn and how much room that gives you today, before you do it. Mark a planned item done and it counts. The In / Out strip on Today shows what has come in, what has gone out and what is left. Live steps from Health Connect (Premium) take over from the plan as soon as they pass it.
💪
Resistance
Log sets, reps, weight and a note per set, or apply a saved routine. Volume (sets × reps × kg) shows on the History day, in Reports and in Statistics. Tap ⧉ to copy the previous set.
📚
Exercise everywhere
Each History day shows its exercise (and resistance sets). The metric chart and Compare Metrics can plot exercise minutes, exercise calories and resistance volume. Reports has an Exercise section: active days, minutes, effort mix, kinds of exercise, weekly minutes, personal bests and the change on the period before. It is also in the AI context, the weekly summary, the clinician view, the carer page and every backup.
🌙
Sleep
Each History day shows a sleep panel: total, a deep / REM / light / awake bar, efficiency, sleeping heart rate and time to fall asleep (whatever your wearable provides). Reports has a Sleep section: average night, nights of 7 hours or more, consistency, the average night by stage, every night as a column, weekday against weekend and the change on the period before. Sleep also reaches the AI, the weekly summary, the clinician view and the carer page.
🧭
What seems to move what (Early Stage)
A Reports card that compares days with a habit against days without it, then looks at what came next: the following night's sleep, deep sleep, sleeping heart rate, next-day HRV and symptoms, and what a short night does to the same day's eating and movement. Habits covered: each kind of exercise, resistance, hard effort, a late last meal, an over-ceiling carb day, a very active day and a logged treatment session. It also looks at bedtime (nights later than your usual against that night's sleep), next-morning weight change and the day of a treatment session plus the two days after. It only shows differences that are both large and clearly beyond ordinary day-to-day wobble, with at least 10 days on each side, and they are associations in your own data, not proof of cause. A small key explains the markers (▲ looks helpful, ▼ looks unhelpful, ● just notable). Not sure what a finding means for you? Tap ✨ What does this mean for me? for a plain-English read of what matters most and what is thin. It uses the same AI route as your other reports (your starter allowance or your own key). It needs a few weeks of data before anything appears.
📊
Statistics
Insights has a Statistics tab between Trends and Reports. ＋ Create a statistic by choosing what to look at (foods, days, weeks, months, days of the week, exercises, kinds of exercise, resistance lifts), what to measure (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, iron, potassium, magnesium, vitamin D, omega-3, steps, sleep and its stages, weight, HRV, exercise minutes and more), total / average / highest / lowest, top 5 to all, any period or custom dates, and how to show it (ranked list, bar chart, line chart, donut or table), with a live preview. Give it a colour and a group. Tick Compare with the period before to see percentage changes and new entries. Every statistic has a ✨ Explain button that says in plain English what the numbers show and what is worth doing about it.
🗂️
Keep, find and share statistics
Statistics sit in groups (Food, Over time, Body, Exercise, My own) with search, a group filter, ▲▼ or the ⠿ handle (drag it) to reorder, 🎨 to change the chart type and colour in a tap, 🙈 to hide (find them under 👁 Hidden), ✎ to edit and 🗑 to delete. 📚 Library offers over twenty ready-made ones to add with a tap. Each can be copied as text or downloaded as CSV. 📤 Share saves your statistics as a small file that holds the questions only, never your data; anyone with MaxedHealth can import it and see the same questions answered from their own history. Everything is in your backup.
📌
Statistics on Today, in reports and in the AI
Tap 📌 on a statistic to pin it to a compact My statistics card on Today (up to four; it stays out of the way until you pin one, and has the usual ▲▼ and 👁). Tick Include in summaries, clinician view and AI and the statistic appears in your weekly summary, the clinician view and the AI context, each with its own period stated. Hidden statistics are never shared.
Where did Top Foods go? The Top Foods and Average by Day of the Week cards used to sit on Trends. They now live on the Statistics tab as the first statistics you start with ("Top 10 foods", "Calories by day of the week"), and you can change them, or build your own.
07 — Protocol

GBM
mode.

MaxedHealth was built by a GBM patient in remission. The protocol support is deep and specific.

⚡
Ketosis badge
Green = within ceiling. Yellow = approaching. Red = exceeded. Always visible on the dashboard.
🎯
Carb ceiling
Set per day-type in Settings — Standard, Occasion, and Holiday each have their own independent limit. Enforced throughout the day with clear warnings when approached or exceeded.
📊
Metabolic analysis
Insights → Reports → AI Brief gives GBM-specific adherence analysis. Trend analysis, Query Builder for specific questions, and Ask AI for open-ended ones (headache patterns, water retention, HRV correlations).
📅
GBM Monthly Summary
A structured monthly review of your GBM protocol — ketosis adherence, metabolic consistency, weight trend, and protocol recommendations. Insights → Reports.
🔬
GBM Research Digest
A persistent, dated home for real research findings — metabolic, dietary, repurposed-drug approaches — colour-coded Proven/Early Stage/Speculative. One-tap "Copy Research Request" pastes a ready-made prompt into a real AI chat, since this app's own AI calls have no live web search. Insights → Reports.
📖
Full protocol guide
Diet, supplements, repurposed drugs, evidence categories. Written by a patient for patients. Read it →
On holiday days: going over the ceiling occasionally doesn't break the protocol. Use Holiday mode for planned higher days. What matters is the trend over weeks, not one number.
07b — Make it yours

Show only
what you need.

MaxedHealth does a lot. You don't have to see all of it at once.

Four small controls sit at the top of Today, Trends, Reports and Settings.
Filter
The topic chips (All, Me & targets and so on) are always visible just under the toolbar. Filter opens the Hidden list: tap a hidden card to bring it back.
Fold
Open all / Close all unfold or fold every card in view. A folded card shrinks to one line that still shows its live value; tap it to open.
Reset
↺ Default puts that screen back to its standard layout. Settings → Manage → Reset layout on every screen does the same everywhere. Nothing you have logged is ever touched.
Voice
Say or type "Hide the fasting card", "Show the ketosis card", "Close all cards" or "Reset my layout" in the Log box.
Starter packs: a head start for your situation.
A pack is a small file of ready-made lists (such as symptoms and treatments) and a screen layout, with nothing about your health and no entries. The setup wizard offers a starting point at first run: Brain tumour tracking, Keep it simple or Show me everything. Change your mind later with Settings → Manage → Setup → Change my focus: pick another starting point, read the preview, and the screens re-arrange (nothing you logged changes). More than twenty condition packs (add a second one with Add as another focus; with two or more conditions, chips on Compare Metrics let you look at one at a time) (for example epilepsy, diabetes, migraine, asthma, arthritis, Parkinson's, menopause, thyroid, sleep, weight loss) are under Settings → Data → Starter packs. You can share your own too: tick Screen layout and the lists you want, then Share my setup only.
Planning a meal for later. Type "I'm planning on eating 2 eggs and toast at 1pm", or tap Plan for later on a meal preview. The meal goes into a separate plan under the Today bars, with totals shown as eaten plus planned against your targets. When the time arrives, it asks whether you ate it; only then does it move into your log. Planned food never counts as eaten until you say so (or switch on Trust my plan).
Fasting heads-up. If you track fasting, a plan that would end a fast early gets a one-line note: how long after your last meal it is, and when your goal would be reached. It is information only; nothing is changed or blocked.
Languages. Settings > Profile > Language (or the first onboarding screen) offers English, Spanish, German, French, Turkish, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, Polish and Dutch, each with its flag. A 🌐 button then sits in the corner of every screen with Back to English. Anything not translated stays in English, and medical terms with no safe translation are left alone rather than guessed. Translations are machine-assisted and have not been checked by clinicians, so check anything important in English. For anything still in English, tap the ✨ Translate this screen with AI button next to the flag: it asks first, sends the English text on screen to your AI provider, and remembers the answers on your phone.
Scoring a day you missed. Under the How are you today? card, ＋ Score a past day lets you fill in the last 14 days after the event.
Reports about a tag. A report profile can be about a treatment or about a tag you use (for example "Swim", or "Any Exercise"), and with more than one condition set up you can narrow the list by condition first.
Merge
Add what's missing keeps everything you already have and skips anything with the same name, so nothing is duplicated; it works the same for the starter foods and recipes, for CSV files and for packs someone shares with you, and your own exports never carry repeats. After a merge into the food library the app offers its duplicate finder for near-matches (a typo, different wording). Replace swaps your version of the ticked items for the pack's.
Nudge
If a pack tucked a Today card away, a week later the app offers it back once: Show it, Not now or Don't ask again. It is always optional.
More than one condition? Tags? Before and after.
Several conditions in one app. Someone with cluster headaches who also has a brain tumour, has acupuncture and sees a physio can keep it all in one place. Settings > Manage > Setup > Change my focus has Add as another focus: it keeps what you have, adds the new pack's symptoms and treatments with that condition's label, and combines the screen layouts so a card is only tucked away if both focuses tuck it away. Switch still replaces. Things you added yourself carry no label and always show.
Condition chips. Once you have two or more conditions, chips appear at the top of Compare Metrics, the Treatment Sessions picker and the report wizard. Tick one or several to see only that condition's symptoms and treatments; none ticked means everything.
Tags are data too. The tags you put on a day (for example Treatment: Massage, Exercise: Swim, Occasion tags) can be used like treatments: pick one (or Any Exercise) as the anchor in the before / during / after view, so you can see how sleep, heart rate variability, weight, food or a symptom looked in the days around it. A tag needs at least three days before it shows up. Tags used on three or more days also become 0/1 pills on the Compare Metrics graph, next to your symptom scores. Your own treatments appear as tag chips too, so you do not type them twice.
Manage tags (a link under the occasion picker on Today) lists every tag with how many days it was used. Rename one (or rename it onto another to merge, ignoring capitals) or remove it from every day, so "massage", "Massage" and "Massage session" do not split your numbers.
How are you today? The 1 to 5 scores are kept even on a day you log no food, and show up everywhere your other data does: Compare Metrics, before / after, reports and the Reports analyst. A clinician asking whether exercise, HRV and nutrition affected your headache days is answered from these.
Copy anything you can read. Under every result the app produces (an AI answer, a report, an import result, a log, a command) there are two small buttons: Select all and 📋 Copy. Copy puts exactly that text on your clipboard, ready to paste into an email or a message to your doctor.
08 — Protect your data

Back up.
Seriously.

MaxedHealth stores everything in Chrome's localStorage — fast and private. But if Chrome clears its storage, it's gone.

If you're on Local mode, most of this is automatic now.
Chrome can clear localStorage when your device runs low on storage, when you clear browsing data, or during a reset. Don't assume it's safe because it was there yesterday — Premium backs up for you automatically; in the free web app, export a backup regularly.
Auto
Full daily backup (Local mode). The first time you open the app each day, everything — history, library, recipes, settings, today's log — backs up automatically to your local server. 7-day rotation. Nothing to remember, nothing to tap. Check Settings → Manage → Data & Backup for confirmation this is active. If you ever need it back — Chrome got cleared, you're setting the app up fresh — Settings → Import → Restore from Server Backup lets you pick a date and load it straight in.
Anytime
Immediate backup. Settings → Import → Full App State → select it in the list → Export selected. Downloads a copy locally *and* pushes an up-to-the-minute version to the server, rather than waiting for tomorrow's automatic one. Also the only backup layer that exists at all for cloud/GitHub Pages users, since there's no server to back up to automatically.
Auto
Premium backups. Premium keeps rotating backups of your data on your own phone automatically.
Monthly
Keep a copy off your phone. Every backup above lives on the phone itself, so a lost, broken or reset phone takes them with it. About once a month, go to Settings → Data, tick what you want and tap Share… (it makes one zip and opens your phone's share menu; Save to Downloads just saves it on the phone) and send it somewhere that is not the phone: email it to yourself, put it in a cloud drive, or copy it to your computer. If you ever need it, Settings → Data → Restore from backup loads it straight back in. Before a big change (a new phone, a factory reset, clearing Chrome), do it that day.
09 — Questions

Things people
ask.

I logged a meal but the macros look wrong. How do I fix it?
Tap the meal in today's log and edit the values. Save the corrected version to your Library with locked values — the AI uses your numbers next time, not its estimate.
What's the difference between typing/voice and the Library?
Typing, voice, photo and barcode all use AI to identify macros from scratch — good for anything new. The Library stores foods you eat regularly with locked values — one tap to log, no AI needed, no drift.
Will I lose my data if I clear Chrome?
On Local mode, no — not anymore. A daily backup of everything (today's log, full history, library, recipes, routines) sits on your server as a JSON file, and Settings → Import → Restore from Server Backup lets you pick a date and load it straight back into the app. A few seconds, not manual file surgery.

Worth knowing: it's a full replacement, not a merge — anything logged since that backup was made (and not also in the backup) won't survive a restore. So it's genuinely there to recover from Chrome being cleared, not a casual daily undo.

On Cloud, there's no server at all, so this doesn't apply — clearing Chrome there is genuinely final unless you've exported. Either way, for extra peace of mind: Settings → Import → Full App State → Export selected any time, which gets you an actual file in Downloads you can see and keep yourself.
Why does the Trends tab show no data?
Trends needs wearable data. Meal logging alone won't populate it. Premium syncs it automatically; in the free web app, type values on Manual Entry or import a file from the Import tab.
Can I use MaxedHealth on multiple devices?
Not automatically — data lives locally on each device. Settings → Import lets you move Library, Supplements, Recipes, Routines and Strength Log between devices (export from one, import on the other). Today's log and full history can now be restored too, but specifically from your own device's own server backup — On Premium, Restore from Server Backup pulls from the phone you're connected to, not from a file you can hand to a different device. Moving that part to a genuinely different phone still means manually copying the backup JSON file over yourself.
Is my data sent anywhere?
Only meal descriptions go to Claude AI for macro identification. Nothing else leaves your device — no accounts, no cloud storage of your personal data, no tracking, ever, on either mode.

"Cloud mode" is a slightly confusing name worth clarifying: it means the *app itself* is loaded from GitHub Pages rather than your own phone's server — not that your data gets uploaded anywhere. On Cloud, your log and history live only in that browser's local storage on whichever device you're using. On Local, they also save to your own phone's server, which is what enables the daily automatic backup and wearable sync. Neither sends your personal health data to Pete Spence, to Anthropic, or to any third party beyond that one meal-description call to Claude.
Will I run out of AI requests on the shared account?
Very unlikely for normal use. The shared account (used automatically unless you add your own API key) is capped at 60 calls per day, purely to keep it fair since everyone shares it. Logging every meal and snack — text, voice, or photo — is roughly one call each, so a typical day of eating comes to somewhere around 5-10. Even a heavier day with several extra AI reports or questions on top rarely gets close to 60. It exists to stop the shared account being drained by one person, not to constrain how you actually use the app day to day. If you ever do hit it, adding your own API key (Settings → Manage → AI Provider) removes the cap entirely, at your own cost instead.
What does "that's me done" actually do?
It gives you an immediate summary and pushes today's totals to the server right away, rather than waiting for the automatic midnight rollover to do it. It also stamps today's weigh-in onto the day's History entry. It isn't required — the day gets saved to History automatically at midnight either way — but it's a quick way to get confirmation and make sure your server backup is current without waiting.
How do I change my carb ceiling?
Settings → Manage → Carb Ceilings. Set different ceilings for standard days, occasions, and holidays independently — GBM/epilepsy's therapeutic reference is ≤20g, but the number itself is fully yours to set.
How does the Resistance activity fill in its own minutes?
It accounts for any strength training logged that day, not resistance bands specifically — Bench Press, Bicep Curl, Rowing, bodyweight Press Ups, whatever's under Strength Training. The moment a set is logged, it syncs automatically so those calories count toward today's total without a separate manual entry.

If no session duration was logged, one gets estimated at ~2.5 minutes per set — a rough real-world average, not measured from you specifically — with effort defaulting to Moderate for the same reason. Both the minutes and effort dropdown are fully editable on the Today tab; correcting either only overrides that day's estimate, never how future sessions get estimated. The same explanation is one tap away in the app itself — look for the ⓘ next to the sync hint.
Why are there two separate "Browse saved prompts" lists?
The Log tab and the Reports/Insights tab each have their own version because they genuinely see different data — the Log tab only ever has today's numbers, Reports has your full history (now including today too). A saved prompt is tagged Today, History, or Both, and only shows in the matching tab's list, so you never accidentally run a question that needs history through the tab that can't see it.

Set the tag yourself via the toggle when saving a prompt (it suggests one automatically based on the words in your question — "today"/"remaining" suggests Today, "recent"/"trend"/"compare" suggests History — but you can always override it). Prompts saved before this existed show up in both lists until you classify them; a one-tap Auto-classify button appears in the manager whenever any are still unclassified.
Can I edit a saved routine directly?
Yes — tap ✏️ Edit next to any routine (alongside Apply/Copy/Delete) to open the same add/remove-exercise form pre-filled with its current list. Save changes updates it in place. Copy still works as before if you want to start a new routine from an existing one instead — it never touches the original.
Can I track how many tablets I'm actually taking, not just the dose?
Yes — each supplement has an optional Tablets field, separate from the dose (e.g. dose "2400mg", tablets "2 capsules"). Set it when adding or editing a supplement in Settings → Manage Supplements, and it shows alongside the dose everywhere that supplement appears.
Can I reorder my supplement lists?
Yes — Settings → Manage Supplements is grouped by time of day (Morning/Midday/Evening/Bedtime) with ▲▼ buttons to reorder within each group, and Today's checklist follows whatever order you set. A supplement taken in more than one period (like Omega-3 or Curcumin) can sit in a different position in each list — reordering one doesn't force the same order everywhere.
I'm reporting a bug to Claude — what's the easiest way to send the relevant debug info?
Settings → Manage → 📋 Copy Diagnostics for Claude. It lists every diagnostic source with a live count of how many entries each one has, ticks the ones with something to report automatically (untick or tick any manually), and one tap copies everything selected — app version, timestamp, and each source's full detail — ready to paste straight into a chat.
10 — Reference

Glossary.

master.csv
Nutrition history file. One row per day, pipe-delimited. Built from end-of-day close-out rows. Keep this backed up.
combined.csv
One row per day of merged wearable data. Import it to populate Trends.
Carb ceiling
Daily carbohydrate limit, set per day-type (Standard/Occasion/Holiday) in Settings → Manage → Carb Ceilings. Therapeutic GBM/epilepsy reference is ≤20g, but it's fully user-configurable.
Weight Phase History
Settings → Manage → Profile. Logs intentional weight phases (loss/maintain/gain) with dates, updated automatically when you change goal. Lets AI reports correctly interpret trends — deliberate loss is never flagged as a concern.
Condition History
Same idea as Weight Phase History, for condition instead of goal. Logs automatically whenever you genuinely change condition, so a later switch can't retroactively distort how old data gets judged. Once you've used more than one condition, Ask AI and Full Summary can compare periods naturally — just ask, no filter needed.
Activity Level
Settings → Manage → Profile. Personalises what counts as an Easy/Moderate/Hard walking pace for you specifically — there's no single correct "brisk" speed for everyone, even the AHA and CDC officially disagree on the number. Also auto-switches on its own from your real, sustained step-count trends — a genuine improvement gets a celebration, a decline gets a plain notice. Logged to Activity Level History the same way Condition History works.
Resistance (activity)
Today tab → Activities. Accounts for any strength training logged that day, not bands specifically. Auto-syncs the moment a set is logged; if no duration was given, estimates ~2.5 min/set at Moderate effort — both editable, correcting either only affects that day.
Balance my macros
Log tab chip. The opposite of Next meal idea — starts from what today's macros still need rather than a category you pick, using the same library-only suggestion engine. Says so plainly and offers smaller ideas instead if there's genuinely too little of the day left for a proper meal.
Ketosis badge
Dashboard indicator. Green = within ceiling. Yellow = approaching. Red = exceeded.
Library
Personal food library with locked macro values. One tap to log. Values never drift.
Day mode
Standard / Occasion / Holiday. Sets which ceiling applies and adjusts AI feedback.
AI Brief
Plain-English summary of your nutrition period. GBM patients get metabolic adherence analysis.
Barcode scanner
Tap ▦ in the Log tab's input bar to scan any product barcode. Looks up exact nutritional values from Open Food Facts.
Label mode
Photo mode for reading nutrition labels. Attach a photo, switch to 🏷 Label, type the product name and amount. AI reads the per-100g column and scales to your stated amount.
Recipe
A saved multi-ingredient meal with a set number of servings. Log one portion with a single tap from the Library tab.
Missed day
History tab → + Missed day. Log a day you forgot to track — describe meals for AI calculation, or enter totals manually.
Fibre
Tracked automatically from barcode scans and food database searches. Set your daily target in Settings. NHS recommendation is 30g/day.
Water tracking
Log water intake from the Today tab's water card. Tap + Glass, + Can, + Bottle or + Custom. Set your daily target in Settings (default 2000ml).
Close-out
Say "that's me done" for an immediate summary and server push, plus stamping today's weigh-in onto the History entry. Optional — the day saves to History automatically at midnight either way.
