Medical Disclaimer

Last updated: DATE

MaxedHealth is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It is a personal health-tracking and information tool. Nothing in this app — including AI-generated responses, reports, or suggestions — is a diagnosis, a treatment plan, or a substitute for advice from a qualified doctor, dietitian, or other healthcare professional.

MaxedHealth helps you log nutrition, weight, activity, sleep, and other health-related data, and uses AI to summarise and comment on that data. Some features are tailored to specific conditions (including glioblastoma, epilepsy, and migraine) based on published research the app cites where relevant — but tailoring a feature to a condition is not the same as diagnosing, treating, or managing that condition on your behalf.

Always consult a professional

Before making any change to your diet, medication, supplement use, or treatment based on anything you see in this app, speak to your doctor or another qualified healthcare provider. This is especially important if you have a serious or complex medical condition, are pregnant or breastfeeding, are on medication that interacts with diet (including anticonvulsants, which can interact meaningfully with ketogenic diets), or are otherwise under active medical supervision.

AI-generated content

Reports, summaries, and chat responses are generated by third-party AI models based on the data you've logged. AI can be wrong, can miss context a human clinician would catch, and does not know your full medical history unless you've told it. Treat AI output as a starting point for a conversation with your doctor, never as a replacement for one.

Emergencies

MaxedHealth is not monitored in real time and is not for use in a medical emergency. If you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact emergency services immediately (999 in the UK, 911 in the US, or your local equivalent).

Privacy Policy

Last updated: DATE

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use MaxedHealth. The short version: your health data stays on your own device unless you explicitly send it to the AI features, in which case only what's needed to answer your question is sent to a third-party AI provider — nothing is stored on a central MaxedHealth server, because there isn't one.

What data MaxedHealth handles

  • Nutrition logs, weight, body composition, activity, sleep, heart rate, and other wearable data you connect
  • Condition, treatment, and symptom information you choose to enter
  • Anything you type or say into the chat/Ask AI features

Some of this — particularly condition and health data — is "special category data" under UK GDPR, which is why it's treated carefully below.

Where your data lives

MaxedHealth runs as a local-first app. Depending on how you've set it up:

  • Local mode — your data lives in a private server running on your own phone (via Termux) and in your browser's local storage. Nobody else, including the developer, can see it. Backups stay on your own device.
  • Cloud mode — your data lives in your browser's local storage only, with no server component at all.

In neither case does the developer operate a central database of user health records. There is no MaxedHealth server holding your logs.

What does leave your device

When you use an AI feature (Ask AI, reports, chat, photo/label recognition), the relevant text or image is sent through a proxy (a Cloudflare Worker operated by the developer) to a third-party AI provider — currently Anthropic (Claude) / Google (Gemini) / OpenAI — CONFIRM WHICH ARE LIVE — to generate a response. CONFIRM: the proxy does not log or retain the contents of these requests beyond what's needed to return the response, and does not associate requests with any persistent user identity. Each AI provider processes the request under its own privacy terms as a data processor.

Your rights

Because your data lives on your own device, you already control access, correction, and deletion directly — through the app's own Export, Backup, and Delete features. If you've contacted the developer directly with a query that included personal data (e.g. a support email), you can ask for that to be deleted at any time.

Children

MaxedHealth is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16 without a parent or guardian's involvement.

Changes to this policy

If MaxedHealth's data handling changes (for example, moving to a paid/hosted model with server-side storage), this policy will be updated and the change will be dated above.
