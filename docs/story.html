01 — Origin

Where this
came from.

The diagnosis
March 2023

I'm a software architect and database developer. I was diagnosed with MGMT-methylated Glioblastoma Multiforme (GBM), the most aggressive form of brain cancer.

After surgery, radiotherapy, and chemotherapy, I was left with something most patients aren't: a technical skillset, a research mindset, and a determination to take every possible measure to support my own recovery.

One of those measures was therapeutic ketosis — keeping daily carbohydrates below 50g to place sustained metabolic pressure on cancer cells that depend heavily on glucose. The evidence base for this in GBM is growing, and it became a non-negotiable part of my daily protocol.

But tracking it precisely — every day — while monitoring weight, HRV, sleep quality, and activity from multiple wearables, and connecting all of that data meaningfully, was beyond what any existing app could do.

"I didn't want an app that tracked my steps. I wanted to know whether yesterday's calories went to fat or muscle. I wanted to know if my ketosis was holding. I wanted my data to work for me — not sit in someone else's cloud."

So I built MaxedHealth. A web app that runs in your browser with no account required, no subscription, and no data leaving your device. The AI handles meal logging. The dashboard connects it all.

I've been using it daily ever since — in remission, still tracking, still improving. And now I'm giving it away.

03 — What it does

Your data,
working for you.

MaxedHealth is a nutrition tracker that connects to your wearable data. Log meals by typing, describing, or photographing them — the AI identifies macros instantly. A personal food library stores your regular foods with locked values, so one tap logs them precisely every time.

Tested with Withings, RingConn, Amazfit/Zepp, and Garmin — but the underlying pipeline works with any export that gives one row per day, so other wearables can likely be supported too. The dashboard plots weight, sleep, HRV, SpO2, steps, and body composition — all alongside your nutrition. One tap syncs everything from your wearable exports.

The question MaxedHealth is designed to answer: what is your eating actually doing to your body?

04 — Why free

Free for you.
Not free for me.

MaxedHealth runs on GitHub Pages (free hosting), so there's no server infrastructure to maintain. But the AI itself isn't free to run — every meal logged, every report generated, goes through a real API that costs money per use, on top of the development-tier access needed to build something like this in the first place. Those costs land on me, not on anyone using the app.

It's free to use because I've chosen to absorb that cost myself, rather than charge people for a tool that helps them manage a condition like mine.

05 — Privacy

Your data.
Full stop.

No data is collected. No analytics. No advertising. No third party ever sees what you log. Six principles. No exceptions.

📱
Stays on your device
All nutrition history, weight logs, and settings live in your browser's localStorage. Nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly export it.
🤖
AI requests are text-only
When you log a meal, only the meal description travels to Claude AI via Cloudflare. Your health data never leaves your device.
🔑
No account, ever
No login, no email, no password. Nobody to be hacked, no database of your records, no company holding your health data.
📡
No tracking
No Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no pixel tracking, no ad network. MaxedHealth has no idea how many people use it.
🔓
Open source
The entire codebase is on GitHub. Read every line, verify every claim, fork it, self-host it, or build on it.
🚫
No upsells
No premium tier, no "unlock with Pro", no advertising. The app you see is the whole app. Forever.
Honest tradeoffs
No dedicated support team — if something breaks, raise a GitHub issue or wait for the next update.
No native iOS app — MaxedHealth runs as a Progressive Web App in Chrome. Very capable, but not the App Store.
Wearable pipeline requires one-time setup — Termux on Android, about 5 minutes. After that, one tap syncs everything.
No automatic cross-device sync — data lives on the device you use. Export and import to transfer.

If you need hand-held onboarding and a live support chat, a paid app may suit you better. If you're technically comfortable and want something that genuinely respects your data and your intelligence — MaxedHealth was built for you.

06 — Get it

Open it.
Right now.

MaxedHealth runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no account, no subscription.

Start here — open in Chrome

Open Chrome on your Android phone and go to:

pete-maxhealth.github.io/maxhealth/maxhealth.html

✓ No API key required — AI is built in and free to use.

The app will redirect automatically to your local server if it's running. Use Chrome's Add to Home Screen to install it — it opens directly, always on the right URL, no browser bar.

Optional — Wearable data (one-time setup)

To connect wearable devices and enable one-tap sync, set up the data pipeline. Takes about 5 minutes.

Samsung Galaxy users: go to Settings → Security and Privacy → Auto Blocker and turn it off before installing. Turn it back on after setup.
Step 1 — Install F-Droid from f-droid.org in Chrome.
Step 2 — Install Termux and Termux:Boot from F-Droid. Tap the Termux:Boot icon once to activate it.
Step 3 — Open Termux and run:
curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pete-maxhealth/maxhealth/main/setup.sh | bash
Step 4 — Restart your phone. From now on, the server starts automatically on every reboot — no Termux needed.
Step 5 — Export from your wearable app, then open MaxedHealth → Import tab → tap SYNC NOW. Done.
