Where this came from

In March 2023, Pete — a software architect and database developer — was diagnosed with MGMT-methylated Glioblastoma Multiforme (GBM), the most aggressive form of brain cancer.

After surgery, radiotherapy, and chemotherapy, Pete was left with something most patients aren't: a technical skillset, a research mindset, and a determination to take every possible measure to support his own recovery.

One of those measures was therapeutic ketosis — keeping daily carbohydrates below 50g to place sustained metabolic pressure on cancer cells that depend heavily on glucose. The evidence base for this in GBM is growing, and for Pete it became a non-negotiable part of his daily protocol.

But tracking it precisely, every day, while also monitoring weight, HRV, sleep quality, and activity from multiple wearables — and connecting all of that data meaningfully — was beyond what any existing app could do.

"I didn't want an app that tracked my steps. I wanted to know whether yesterday's calories went to fat or muscle. I wanted to know if my ketosis was holding. I wanted my data to work for me — not sit in someone else's cloud."

So Pete built MaxedHealth. A web app that runs in your browser with no account required, no subscription, and no data leaving your device. The AI handles meal logging. The dashboard connects it all.

He's been using it daily ever since. And now he's giving it away.

What it does

MaxedHealth is a nutrition tracker that connects to your wearable data. Log meals by typing, describing, or photographing them. The AI identifies macros instantly. A personal food library remembers your regular foods. At the end of every day, one tap copies a formatted row to your nutrition log.

Import data from Withings, RingConn, Garmin, Oura, Amazfit/Zepp, or any device that exports a CSV. The dashboard plots weight trends, sleep quality, HRV, SpO2, steps, and activity — all connected to your nutrition.

The question MaxedHealth is designed to answer: what is my eating actually doing to my body?

How to get it

MaxedHealth runs entirely in your browser. No installation, no account, no subscription.

START HERE — OPEN IN CHROME

Open Chrome on your Android phone and go to this address:

pete-maxhealth.github.io/maxhealth/maxhealth.html

✓ No API key required — AI is built in and free to use.

Bookmark it or use Chrome's Add to Home Screen option to install it like an app. A setup wizard walks you through everything — takes about 2 minutes.

OPTIONAL — WEARABLE DATA (ADVANCED)

To connect wearable devices like Withings, RingConn or Garmin, you'll need to set up the data pipeline. This is a one-time process.

Samsung Galaxy users: Before installing, go to Settings → Security and Privacy → Auto Blocker and turn it off temporarily.
Step 1 — Install F-Droid from f-droid.org in Chrome.
Step 2 — Install Termux and Termux:Boot from F-Droid.
Step 3 — Open Termux and paste this command:
curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pete-maxhealth/maxhealth/main/setup.sh | bash
Step 4 — Export data from your wearable (Withings, RingConn, Amazfit/Zepp, Garmin, or any CSV), run the pipeline, then import combined.csv in the app's Import tab.

A note on privacy

Pete's diagnosis is mentioned here because it matters — it explains why MaxedHealth was built the way it was, and why it might matter to you. But the app itself contains no reference to any individual's medical history. It is a tool. What you track in it is entirely your own.

No data is collected. No analytics. No advertising. No third party ever sees what you log.
