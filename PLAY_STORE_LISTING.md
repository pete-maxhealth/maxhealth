# Google Play listing draft (13 Nov 2026)

Draft text for the store page, ready to paste once a Play Console account exists. Wording follows Google's Health apps policy: no claims to diagnose or treat, a clear medical disclaimer, and only what the app really does. Check each claim against the app before submitting.

**App name (30 max):** MaxedHealth: Food & Goals

**Short description (80 max):** Track food, movement and sleep for your goal. Free, no account.

**Full description (4000 max):**

MaxedHealth is a free tracker for food, movement, sleep, weight and more, set up around what you want to achieve. There is no account to create and no advert to dismiss. Your records stay on your phone.

CHOOSE YOUR GOAL
Start with Lose weight, Keep carbs low, Type 2 diabetes, Build muscle or Just see how I am doing. Each one sets up a calm first screen. Add more only when you want it, and hide anything you do not use.

LOG WITHOUT THE TYPING
Pick meals from your own foods, scan a barcode, photograph a label, or speak what you ate. Typing numbers is the last resort, not the default.

SEE WHAT IS HAPPENING
Daily targets that move with your activity, weekly trends, sleep and weight side by side, and plain explanations of what the numbers mean. Anything suggested is labelled by how strong the evidence is: Proven, Early stage or Speculative.

BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO WANT DETAIL
Carbs counted your way (net, total or gross), fasting windows, supplements, symptoms, lab results, meal timing and reports you can share with a clinician or carer. Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.

TRY IT FIRST
Open a demo person with a month of sample data. Nothing you do in the demo is saved.

YOUR DATA
Stays on your device. AI features (photo and label reading, questions about your own days) send only what you choose to send to an AI provider through a small proxy; the privacy policy says exactly what is sent. You can export or delete everything at any time.

IMPORTANT
MaxedHealth is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition, and it does not replace advice from your doctor or dietitian. Talk to a healthcare professional before changing your diet, exercise or treatment.

Available in English, Spanish, German, French, Turkish, Portuguese, Italian, Polish and Dutch.

## Data safety form: draft answers
- Data collected and shared: **health and fitness data and photos/text are sent to a third-party AI provider only when the user uses an AI feature**, through the developer's proxy; not stored centrally; optional.
- Once a day the app sends a **random device code and the app version** to the proxy, so installs can be counted. Declare as app activity / device identifier used for analytics, not linked to identity.
- Everything else stays on the device. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Users can delete their data in the app (Settings) at any time.
- No account, no ads, no data sold, not designed for children.

## Health apps declaration
Select that the app is a health and fitness / nutrition app that is **not** a medical device and makes no diagnostic claims. Uses Health Connect: **no** in the free app (that is the Premium launcher).
