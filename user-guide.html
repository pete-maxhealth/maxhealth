00 — Getting started

First time
setup.

Takes about 5 minutes. You'll never need to do this again.

⚡ Automatic 👆 Tap required 🔁 One-time only ⏭ Optional
1
Install Termux from F-Droid
👆 Tap required 🔁 One-time
Termux is a Linux terminal for Android. It runs the wearable data pipeline in the background.

Install F-Droid first from f-droid.org, then search for Termux inside it.

Do not use the Play Store version — it's outdated and will fail.
⚠ Samsung Galaxy users: go to Settings → Security → Auto Blocker and turn it off before installing. Turn it back on after.
2
Install Termux:Boot
👆 Tap required 🔁 One-time
Termux:Boot starts the MaxedHealth server automatically on every phone reboot — no Termux interaction needed after setup.

F-Droid → search "Termux:Boot" → install.

After installing, find Termux:Boot in your app drawer and tap it once to activate it. Nothing visible will happen — that's normal.
3
Open Termux and paste the setup command
👆 One tap
Open Termux, tap the screen, and paste:
Termux
curl -sSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pete-maxhealth/maxhealth/main/setup.sh | bash
⚠ A storage permission popup appears within the first few seconds — tap Allow immediately or the script will fail.
4
mhstart is ready to use
⚡ Automatic
The setup script configured mhstart automatically — no extra steps needed.

If the app ever shows a grey dot, open Termux and type mhstart to restart the server instantly.

⚠ Important: Always minimise Termux using the home button — never swipe it away from the recent apps list. Swiping it away kills the server and the app will lose sync until you restart it with mhstart.
5
Wait ~2 minutes
⚡ Automatic
The script installs Python, Git, creates all folders, clones the repo, and configures auto-start. When you see Setup complete! 🎉 — you're done.
6
Restart your phone, then open the app
🔁 One-time
Restart your phone once — this activates Termux:Boot so the server starts automatically from now on.

Then open: localhost:5757

You'll see the onboarding wizard. Enter your name, weight, target, and health profile. Takes 90 seconds. Your goal (gain/lose/maintain) is set automatically from your weights.
Tap the Install → nudge at the top to add MaxedHealth to your home screen — it always opens to localhost:5757 directly, no browser bar.
Just exploring first?
Settings → "Try a demo first" loads real, richly-populated sample data — a full library, 30 days of history, recipes, routines, strength sessions — entirely in memory. Nothing is ever written to real storage during a demo, and exiting restores your actual data untouched, whether or not you've completed onboarding yet.
Keeping it updated
Run the same setup command any time to update. It detects an existing install and pulls latest changes without touching your data.
01 — Core loop

Your daily
workflow.

Four steps. Once it's habit, each takes under a minute.

01
Morning
Log your weight
On the Dash tab, tap UPDATE. Weigh yourself first thing — naked, after the bathroom, before eating. Same conditions every day makes the trend meaningful.
Why it matters: daily weight fluctuates 1–2kg from food, water, and digestion. One number means nothing. Thirty numbers tell the real story.
02
Throughout the day
Log meals as you eat them
Tap the Nutrition tab. There are four ways to log food — use whichever fits the moment:

📝 Type it — describe what you ate in plain English. The AI identifies components and calculates macros automatically.
2 scrambled eggs with butter and smoked salmon chicken thigh 180g with roasted broccoli baked beans on toast
▦ Scan a barcode — tap the barcode button, point at any product. Looks up exact values from Open Food Facts (3M+ products). Enter the amount and log.

🔍 Search the database — tap the search button, type a product or brand name. Select from results, enter grams, done.

🏷 Label photo — tap the camera, switch to Label mode, photograph a nutrition label, type the product name and amount. AI reads the exact per-100g values and scales to your amount.

📷 Meal photo — photograph a plate. AI estimates components and portions. Tap "✏ Correct this" if anything is wrong.

Library: save any food to your Library for instant one-tap future logging. Recipes save multi-ingredient meals — set servings once, log one portion with a tap.
03
End of day
Close out and save your row
When you're done eating, type any of these in the Assistant tab:
that's me done · done for today · all done · wrapping up
MaxedHealth generates your daily summary and automatically saves it to master.csv on your device. No copying, no pasting — it just happens.
Don't skip this. Close-out saves the day to History and writes to master.csv. Without it, the day is saved in memory but not yet committed to file.
04
Throughout the day
Track your water
On the Dash, scroll to the 💧 Water card. Tap + Glass (250ml), + Can (330ml), + Bottle (500ml), or + Custom. The progress bar fills toward your daily target (default 2000ml). When you hit your target it flashes blue.
Water history appears in the History tab and Trends chart after midnight rollover — the same as wearable data.
05
Optional
Set your day mode
Tell the app what kind of day it is before you start logging:

🟢 Standard — full carb ceiling applies
🟡 Occasion — social event, slightly more flexibility
🔴 Holiday — ceiling not enforced

The ketosis badge and AI feedback adjust automatically.
02 — Data files

Two files.
Both matter.

MaxedHealth uses two CSV files for different purposes.

master.csv
Your nutrition history
Your nutrition history — one row per day, written automatically at close-out:
DD/MM/YY|Calories|Protein|Carbs|Notes 09/05/26|2840|173|40|chicken dinner

Auto-saved: close-out writes directly to master.csv — no copying needed.

Missed a day? Go to History tab → + Missed day. Describe what you ate and the AI calculates the totals, or enter them manually.

Backup: Import tab → Backup master.csv to Downloads. Keep a copy somewhere safe.
combined.csv
Your wearable data
Pipeline output. One row per day, merged from all connected devices — steps, sleep, HR, HRV, SpO2, weight.

Source: the pipeline in Termux reads your device exports and writes this file.

Import: Import tab → Load combined.csv.

What it unlocks: the Trends tab — sleep, activity, HR, weight and body composition (muscle mass, fat, bone, hydration) plotted over time alongside your nutrition. Tap any metric card for a full drill-down chart.
02a — At a glance

What the numbers
actually mean.

Not just totals — whether they're good news, given what you're actually trying to do.

🚦
Traffic-light status dots
Small coloured dots next to Calories/Protein/Carbs/Fat on the dashboard. Carbs uses ceiling logic — green comfortably under, red once over. The other three use gaining-phase logic by default: green at or above target, red only if meaningfully under — since under-eating, not exceeding, is the real risk on a surplus-seeking protocol.
🎯
Weight intention status
Reads your actual Goal/Phase setting (Lose/Maintain/Gain) and colours your 14-day weight trend by whether it's actually serving that goal — losing during a Lose phase is green, but gaining is red; losing too fast is amber, not celebrated outright. A no-consequence preview mode in Settings lets you see how it would look under a different goal, without changing anything real.
🏋️
Exercise Offset for carb overage
Off by default (Settings → Exercise Offset). When enabled, distinguishes a carb overage that was meaningfully addressed by logged exercise that day from one that wasn't — never hides or replaces the raw "over ceiling" fact, just adds an honest second layer on top of it.
ℹ️
Info icons on ambiguous features
Small ℹ️ icons next to features that could be confused with a similar one — tap to reveal a brief explanation, tap again to hide. Doesn't clutter the screen by default.
03 — Nutrition tools

Every way to
log food.

Six methods. Use whichever fits — they all feed the same daily total.

▦
Barcode scanner
Tap the barcode button in the Nutrition input bar. Point your camera at any product barcode. MaxedHealth looks it up on Open Food Facts — exact product name, calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre, sugar, salt, iron. Enter the amount in grams and log. Works on almost any packaged food in the UK and worldwide.
🔍
Food database search
Tap the search button. Type a product name, brand or ingredient. Results appear automatically — tap one to select it, enter how many grams you had, and log. Same database as the barcode scanner — 3M+ products.
🏷
Label photo
Take a photo of a nutrition label. Switch to Label mode (it auto-selects when a photo is attached). Type the product name and amount in the text box — e.g. "ASDA Cottage Cheese 150g". The AI reads the exact per-100g values from the label and scales to your stated amount.
Label mode reads the left column on UK labels — always per 100g, never per serving.
📷
Meal photo
Take a photo of your plate. The AI identifies the components and estimates portions. If anything's wrong, tap ✏ Correct this beneath the logged entry — describe the correction and it re-logs automatically.
📚
Library
Save any food to your Library for instant one-tap future logging. Values are confirmed and locked — no AI guessing on repeat items. Tap EDIT to change values or delete. Tap LOG to log instantly. Use the search box at the top to filter by name as you type — it covers meals and ingredients together, with a live count of matches so you know how far you've narrowed things down.
Editing library values only affects future logging — not meals already recorded. To correct a past entry, go to the History tab.
🍳
Recipes vs Meals
Two similar-looking but different tools, both in the Library tab. Recipes are for anything batch-cooked or eaten across multiple sittings — set the number of servings once, then log any portion (1, 2, half) with the maths done for you. Meals (in Food Library) are simpler — they log the whole saved amount every time, for anything you always eat in one go. Each section links to the other, so if you save something in the wrong place it's a quick fix, not a redo.
📚
Library-aware meal suggestions
Tap Next meal idea, then 📚 Check what fits from my library. Rather than a generic suggestion, this checks your actual remaining calories, protein, carbs and fat against your real saved Library items and Recipes — proposing genuine combinations (or a specific number of Recipe servings) that would close today's gap, with real numbers, ready to log in one tap.
If a food you describe doesn't exactly match what's saved (wrong brand, for instance), MaxedHealth offers real alternatives from your own Library instead of guessing fresh — or lets you save it as a genuinely new, separate item if it turns out to be a different product entirely.
03a — Depth

Before you
commit to it.

Voice, and knowing the real impact of a meal before it's logged — not after.

🎤
Voice logging
Tap the mic, speak a meal or a question, and a one-tap "Log it" appears right on the transcript — no separate send button to hunt for. The transcript stays editable too, so a mis-hearing can be caught by hand instead of committed blindly.
⚖️
Impact preview, before logging
Every "Ready to log" screen shows exactly what would happen if you actually log it — calories, protein and fat as before → after against today's real targets, plus a clear ceiling check: still within it, or over by how much, including whether it would end a current streak.
🔍
Ambiguous matches, shown honestly
Type something generic like "mince" and every real match in your library is listed with full macros, not a silent guess at the first one found — with a delete option right there if you spot a genuine duplicate while choosing.
03b — Layout

Make it
yours.

Every section on the Today dashboard, the Library tab, and Settings can be reordered to match how you actually use the app.

▲▼
Reordering sections
Small ▲▼ buttons sit in the header of every reorderable section — tap them to move that section up or down. Your chosen order is remembered and applied every time you open the app. Works on the Today dashboard, the Library tab's Recipes/Food Library split, and the Reports, Manage and Import tabs under Settings/Insights.
04 — Corrections

Made a
mistake?

Every entry is editable. Nothing is permanent until you decide it is.

✏
Edit today's entries
On the Dash, tap any entry in Today's Log. An inline editor opens — change the description, calories, protein or carbs. Tap Save. The daily total updates instantly. Tap × to remove an entry entirely — you have 5 seconds to undo.
📅
Edit previous days
Go to the History tab, expand any day, and tap any entry to edit it. The day totals recalculate automatically. Tap ✏ Edit day totals to override the whole day's figures without editing individual entries.
➕
Log a missed day
Go to the History tab and tap + Missed day. Enter the date, then either describe what you ate (AI calculates the totals) or enter the numbers directly. Saves to History and master.csv automatically.
05 — Movement

Logging
activity.

Exercise calories adjust your daily target. For Walking, Cycling, and any custom activity where it matters, effort is calculated for you.

🚶
Walking & Resistance
On the Dash, scroll to Today's Activity. Tick the box, enter minutes. Walking also asks for distance — enter it and pick km or mi using the toggle next to the box. Resistance only needs minutes; tap Easy / Moderate / Hard yourself.
⚡
Auto-calculated effort
For any activity with a distance field, MaxedHealth works out Easy / Moderate / Hard from your pace and total distance — a long walk at a gentle pace can still land as Hard, the same way it would feel in real life. The dropdown shows the result; a small note underneath confirms it's auto-calculated.
The effort dropdown is always yours to override. Tap it and choose differently any time — your choice sticks until you change the distance or minutes again, at which point it re-calculates fresh.
➕
Adding a custom activity
Tap + Add exercise and name it — Cycling, Rowing, Swimming, Yoga, anything. You'll be asked: "Does this need distance as well as time to calculate effort?" Say yes for anything where pace matters and you'll get the same distance + unit toggle as Walking. Say no and it behaves like Resistance — minutes only, manual effort.
06 — Pipeline

Connecting
wearables.

MaxedHealth reads the export files wearable apps produce. The pipeline merges them into combined.csv.

01
Primary method — no Termux needed
Export, then tap Sync Now
Export from your wearable app — the file lands in Downloads. Then open MaxedHealth → Import tab → tap SYNC NOW.

The app moves the export from Downloads, runs the pipeline, and loads the result automatically. No Termux, no commands, no manual file selection.
Amazfit / Zepp only: the Zepp export is AES-256 encrypted — extract it with ZArchiver (free, Play Store) first, then tap Sync.
02
Manual method
Run the pipeline directly
Copy your export to the inbox, then:
cd /storage/emulated/0/maxhealth/app python update_health.py --device withings python update_health.py --device ringconn python update_health.py --device amazfit
Full guide: Setup Guide ↑
Amazfit / Zepp: the Zepp app exports an encrypted zip. Use ZArchiver (free, Play Store) to extract it to the inbox first. The pipeline detects the extracted folders automatically — no password needed.
03
Automatic after sync
Data loads automatically
After tapping SYNC NOW, combined.csv is loaded into the app automatically — no manual file selection needed. Your Trends and History populate immediately.

If you ran the pipeline manually via Termux, use Import tab → Select File Manually to load it.
07 — Advisor

Your personal
nutrition advisor.

MaxedHealth doesn't just track — it advises. From day one, before you have any history, it can tell you exactly what to eat and why.

⭐
Suggested Targets — day one
Enter height, age, sex, weight, condition and goal in Settings → Profile. The app immediately calculates your personalised TDEE (using your actual step data once available), phase-adjusted calories, protein and fat. One tap applies everything. No guesswork, no generic defaults.
📈
Suggested Targets — with history
As wearable data accumulates, the activity multiplier refines automatically. Targets recalculate when weight updates. GBM/Epilepsy users get a ketogenic ratio check (fat ≥65% of calories) built in.
🔄
Phase-aware throughout
Switch goal between Loss, Maintain and Gain — targets recalculate instantly and a phase entry is automatically logged with today's date. AI reports know which phase applied on which dates, so weight trends are always interpreted correctly. Deliberate loss is never flagged as a concern.
⭐
Full Summary
One tap in Insights → Reports → Ask AI generates a 9-section health analysis covering nutrition, weight phases, ketosis quality, sleep, HRV, activity, best periods, areas to improve and a protocol verdict. Phase-aware, condition-specific, nil days excluded.
Two-stage value. Immediate advice at setup — no history needed. Compounding insight over time as data grows. Most apps just log. MaxedHealth tells you what to eat and then checks whether it worked.
08 — Analysis

Reports
& Trends.

Available once you have data. The longer you track, the more useful these become.

📈
Trends
Weight, sleep, HR, HRV, SpO2, steps, and body composition (muscle, fat, bone, hydration) — over time alongside nutrition. Requires combined.csv. Tap any Full Chart to see the complete history.
📋
Summary cards
Average macros, adherence %, streak, and best day — for any date range. Day-type aware: holiday/occasion days evaluated against their own ceilings, never penalised against standard targets. Nil days excluded from all calculations.
🔍
Query builder
"Days under protein target?" · "Days over carb ceiling?" · "Current streak?" Answered instantly from all 39 data columns.
⭐
Full Summary (Ask AI)
One tap generates a comprehensive phase-aware, condition-specific analysis. Or ask any specific question — the AI has your full nutrition, sleep, HRV, steps and weight history.
🗺️
Your Journey
Weight across your entire tracked history, always — independent of the Today/30/60/All filter above it. Each point coloured by that day's ketosis status: green for within ceiling, and if Exercise Offset is enabled (Settings), a third colour for "over ceiling but meaningfully addressed by exercise" vs genuinely unaddressed. Treatment days marked along the bottom.
🧪
Treatment Analysis
Nutrition, ketosis adherence, and weight compared on treatment-tagged days vs standard days — plus detected treatment cycles and a specific comparison against the immediate 7-day recovery window after each one. Based on occasion tags in your history.
🌾
Carb Pattern breakdown
What % of your days were Strict Keto / Keto / Low Carb (or the equivalent neutral tiers for non-keto conditions) — framed around hitting your own chosen target, not a ranking against strict keto specifically.
🔬
GBM Research Digest
A persistent, dated home for real research findings — GBM condition only. Ask Claude to research current developments in a normal chat conversation, paste the result in, and it's saved here with the same Proven / Early Stage / Speculative colour-coding used in Monthly Summary. Deliberately not automated inside the app — an AI call with no real web access would risk fabricating citations, which isn't acceptable for anything health-related.
📐
Formulas & Technical Reference
Every calculation the app actually uses, in plain language — Mifflin-St Jeor BMR, TDEE activity tiers, MET-based exercise calories, macro ratios, carb ceiling logic. Settings → Manage. No black boxes.
08 — Protocol

Your condition.
Your framing.

MaxedHealth was built by a GBM patient, but it works for anyone following a structured nutrition protocol. Set your condition in Settings → Profile and all AI reports adapt to your situation.

🧬
GBM / Epilepsy
Therapeutic ketogenic protocol. Reports use evidence-based framing with [Proven] / [Early Stage] / [Speculative] categorisation. Carb compliance evaluated against 20–30g therapeutic target. GBM reports include gaining phase and oncology context.
⚡
Strict Ketosis
Standard ketogenic 20–50g. Reports focus on metabolic health, fat adaptation and weight progress. No oncology framing.
💉
Type 1 Diabetes
Carb-awareness focus. Reports flag insulin dosing implications of carb changes. Evidence: Early Stage (Bernstein method, Lennerz et al 2018).
📉
Type 2 Diabetes
Low carb 50–100g target. Reports framed around glucose stability, HbA1c impact and medication review. Evidence: Proven (Virta Health, Lean et al 2018).
⚖️
General Health / Weight Loss
Balanced low carb 100–150g. Reports focus on calorie deficit, trend consistency and sustainable habits.
📊
Day-type aware
Holiday and Occasion days have their own carb ceilings. Reports evaluate compliance against each day's actual ceiling — a holiday day within its limit is fully compliant and never penalised against standard targets.
Carb ceilings are separate from your condition setting. Your condition tells the AI how to frame advice. Your ceilings (set in Settings → Carb Ceilings) control the actual limits. Change either independently.
09 — Protect your data

Back up.
Seriously.

MaxedHealth stores everything in Chrome's localStorage — fast and private. But if Chrome clears its storage, it's gone.

Three habits. That's it.
Chrome can clear localStorage when your device runs low on storage, when you clear browsing data, or during a reset. Don't assume it's safe because it was there yesterday.
Weekly
Export your JSON backup. Settings → Export Backup Now. Saves everything — history, library, settings, targets. Save to Google Drive or email to yourself.
Daily
Keep master.csv. Copy your daily row to a plain text file after every close-out. Works independent of the app — readable anywhere.
Auto
Pipeline backups. Every pipeline run backs up combined.csv and nutrition.csv automatically. Up to 7 rotating backups at /storage/emulated/0/maxhealth/data/backup/
10 — Questions

Things people
ask.

I logged a meal but the macros look wrong. How do I fix it?
Tap the meal in today's log and edit the values. Save the corrected version to your Library with locked values — the AI uses your numbers next time, not its estimate.
What's the difference between the Assistant tab and the Library?
Assistant uses AI to identify macros from a description — good for anything new. The Library stores foods you eat regularly with locked values — one tap to log, no AI needed, no drift.
Will I lose my data if I clear Chrome?
Yes — clearing Chrome's site data wipes localStorage. Export regularly: Settings → Export Backup Now. Keep master.csv backed up separately. This is the most important thing in this guide.
Why does the Trends tab show no data?
Trends needs combined.csv from the pipeline. Meal logging alone won't populate it. Connect a wearable via the pipeline, or import a combined.csv from the Import tab.
Can I use MaxedHealth on multiple devices?
Not automatically — data lives locally on each device. Export from one (Settings → Export All Data) and import on another to transfer everything.
Is my data sent anywhere?
Only meal descriptions go to Claude AI for macro identification. Nothing else leaves your device. No accounts, no cloud storage, no tracking — ever.
What does "that's me done" actually do?
It triggers the end-of-day close-out. MaxedHealth calculates your daily totals, generates a summary, produces a master.csv row, and saves the day to History. Without it, the day isn't recorded.
How do I change my carb ceiling?
Settings → Carb Ceilings. Set different ceilings for standard days, occasions, and holidays independently. GBM protocol default is 50g standard.
The Import tab shows a grey dot — what does that mean?
The local server has stopped running. This is usually harmless — Android occasionally kills background processes to free memory. Open Termux and type mhstart to restart it instantly. The server starts automatically on every phone reboot via Termux:Boot.

Tip: Always minimise Termux with the home button — never swipe it away. Swiping kills the server.
11 — Reference

Glossary.

master.csv
Nutrition history file. One row per day, pipe-delimited. Built from end-of-day close-out rows. Keep this backed up.
combined.csv
Pipeline output. One row per day of merged wearable data. Import to populate Trends.
Inbox
Drop wearable exports here for the pipeline. /storage/emulated/0/maxhealth/data/inbox/
Pipeline
Python scripts in Termux that read device exports and produce combined.csv.
Sync Now
The one-tap sync button in the Import tab. Moves exports from Downloads to inbox, runs the pipeline, and loads the result automatically. No Termux required.
Carb ceiling
Daily carbohydrate limit. GBM default: 50g. Set in Settings → Carb Ceilings.
Ketosis badge
Dashboard indicator. Green = within ceiling. Yellow = approaching. Red = exceeded.
Library
Personal food library with locked macro values. One tap to log. Values never drift.
Day mode
Standard / Occasion / Holiday. Sets which ceiling applies and adjusts AI feedback.
AI Brief
Plain-English summary of your nutrition period. GBM patients get metabolic adherence analysis.
mhstart
Termux shortcut to safely restart the local server. Type it any time the app shows a grey dot. Set up once during installation. Always minimise Termux — never swipe it away.
Barcode scanner
Tap ▦ in the Nutrition bar to scan any product barcode. Looks up exact nutritional values from Open Food Facts.
Label mode
Photo mode for reading nutrition labels. Attach a photo, switch to 🏷 Label, type the product name and amount. AI reads the per-100g column and scales to your stated amount.
Recipe
A saved multi-ingredient meal with a set number of servings. Log one portion with a single tap from the Library tab.
Missed day
History tab → + Missed day. Log a day you forgot to track — describe meals for AI calculation, or enter totals manually.
Fibre
Tracked automatically from barcode scans and food database searches. Set your daily target in Settings. NHS recommendation is 30g/day.
Effort (Easy/Moderate/Hard)
For Walking and any custom activity you've flagged as distance-based, calculated automatically from pace and total distance rather than guessed from speed alone — a long walk at a gentle pace can still register as Hard. Always overridable by tapping the dropdown yourself.
Water tracking
Log water intake from the Dash water card. Tap + Glass, + Can, + Bottle or + Custom. Set your daily target in Settings (default 2000ml).
Close-out
Say "that's me done". Generates daily totals, master.csv row, saves day to History.
