Phone-to-phone transfer: test

Prototype only. Nothing here touches your MaxedHealth data. It sends sample data (a 600-food library, 60 recipes and 300 stock changes) from one phone to another by showing QR codes. No network, no server.

Use the same page on both phones. Open Send on one, Receive on the other, and hold the receiving camera steady about 20 cm from the screen.
