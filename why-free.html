The Short Answer

It was built for personal use, by someone who needed it.

MaxedHealth was built by Pete — a software architect and Oracle DBA living with GBM (glioblastoma), a grade 4 brain tumour. He needed a tool that could track therapeutic ketosis with the precision his condition demanded, connect wearable data to nutrition, and give him an AI assistant that understood the protocol. No app existed that did all of this. So he built one.

Once it existed, making it free cost nothing extra. The app runs on GitHub Pages (free), the AI runs through a Cloudflare proxy (negligible cost), and there are no servers to maintain. Charging for it would add complexity — billing systems, accounts, support — that would take time away from building features.

It is free because it cost nothing to make it free.

How Your Data Is Handled
📱
Stays on your device
All nutrition history, weight logs, and settings are stored in your browser's localStorage. Nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly export it.
🤖
AI requests are text-only
When you log a meal by text or photo, only the meal description travels to Claude AI via a Cloudflare proxy. Your health data never leaves your device.
🔑
No account, ever
There is no login, no email, no password, no user ID. There is nobody to be hacked, no database of your information, no company holding your health records.
📡
No tracking or analytics
No Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no pixel tracking, no ad network. MaxedHealth has no idea how many people use it or what they log.
🔓
Open source
The entire codebase is on GitHub. You can read every line of code, verify every claim on this page, fork it, self-host it, or build on it.
🚫
No ads, no upsells
There is no premium tier, no "unlock with Pro", no advertising. The app you see is the whole app. Forever.
The Honest Tradeoffs

What you give up compared to a polished commercial app

No dedicated support team — if something breaks, you raise a GitHub issue or wait for the next update. No native iOS/Android app — it runs as a Progressive Web App in Chrome (very capable, but not the App Store). No automatic sync across devices — data lives on the device you use. The wearable pipeline requires a one-time Termux setup on Android, which takes about 10 minutes and requires following instructions.

These are real limitations. If you need hand-held onboarding and a support chat, a paid app may suit you better. If you're technically comfortable and want something that genuinely respects your data and your intelligence — MaxedHealth was built for you.
