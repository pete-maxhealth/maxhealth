MaxedHealth / Why is it free?

It's free because
it was built for me.

MaxedHealth started as a personal tool. Sharing it with others in the same situation costs nothing extra — so there's no reason to charge.

The full story
🧠

Built by a patient, for patients

I was diagnosed with GBM (glioblastoma) in 2023. Managing metabolic health became central to my treatment strategy — tracking carbs, protein, fat, supplements, sleep, HRV, and weight every single day.

I built MaxedHealth because nothing off the shelf did what I needed. It's designed around a strict therapeutic ketogenic protocol, not casual calorie counting.

👨‍💻

It already exists — sharing is free

The app runs on your own device, on your own server, through your own Termux installation. There's no cloud infrastructure for me to pay for, no servers to maintain, no database to run.

The code is already written. Letting someone else use it costs me nothing.

🤝

Community matters more than money

If you're here, you're probably navigating something serious. The last thing you need is a subscription standing between you and a tool that might help.

I'd rather have five people using it properly than fifty who signed up and never configured it.

🔒

No ads, no tracking, no upsells

MaxedHealth doesn't have a business model. It doesn't serve ads, doesn't sell your data, doesn't have a "Pro" tier, and never will. Your health data stays on your device.

The AI features route through a shared Cloudflare proxy so you don't need your own API key — that's the only external service involved, and it exists purely for convenience.

What this means for you
✅

Free. Permanently.

There's no trial period, no premium version being held back, and no plans to monetise. If you're finding it useful, that's enough.

If something's broken or a feature would genuinely help your situation, raise it. This is built by a real person who uses it every day — feedback goes directly into the next version.
