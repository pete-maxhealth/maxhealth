01 — The short answer

It cost nothing
to make it free.

Built for personal use, by someone who needed it.

MaxedHealth was built by Pete — a software architect and Oracle DBA living with GBM (glioblastoma), a grade 4 brain tumour. He needed a tool that could track therapeutic ketosis with the precision his condition demanded, connect wearable data to nutrition, and give him an AI assistant that understood the protocol. No app existed that did all of this. So he built one.

Once it existed, making it free cost nothing extra. The app runs on GitHub Pages (free), the AI runs through a Cloudflare proxy (negligible cost), and there are no servers to maintain. Charging for it would add complexity — billing systems, accounts, support — that would take time away from building features.

It is free because it cost nothing to make it free.

03 — Your data

How your data
is handled.

Six principles. No exceptions.

📱
Stays on your device
All nutrition history, weight logs, and settings live in your browser's localStorage. Nothing leaves your phone unless you explicitly export it.
🤖
AI requests are text-only
When you log a meal, only the meal description travels to Claude AI via Cloudflare. Your health data never leaves your device.
🔑
No account, ever
No login, no email, no password. Nobody to be hacked, no database of your records, no company holding your health data.
📡
No tracking
No Google Analytics, no Mixpanel, no pixel tracking, no ad network. MaxedHealth has no idea how many people use it.
🔓
Open source
The entire codebase is on GitHub. Read every line, verify every claim, fork it, self-host it, or build on it.
🚫
No upsells
No premium tier, no "unlock with Pro", no advertising. The app you see is the whole app. Forever.
04 — Honest tradeoffs

What you give up
compared to paid.

These are real limitations. Worth knowing.

No dedicated support team — if something breaks, you raise a GitHub issue or wait for the next update. No native iOS/Android app — it runs as a Progressive Web App in Chrome, which is very capable but not the App Store. No automatic sync across devices — data lives on the device you use. The wearable pipeline requires a one-time Termux setup on Android, which takes about 10 minutes.

If you need hand-held onboarding and a live support chat, a paid app may suit you better. If you're technically comfortable and want something that genuinely respects your data and your intelligence — MaxedHealth was built for you.
