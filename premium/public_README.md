# MaxedHealth

A free, private health and nutrition tracker that runs in your browser: log meals (by typing, describing or photographing them), follow your trends, build reports for your clinician, and explore the app with 28 example people before you enter anything of your own. It comes with 27 starter packs for common conditions and goals, and works in English, Spanish, German, French and Turkish.

**Open it:** https://pete-maxhealth.github.io/maxhealth/maxhealth.html (add it to your home screen to use it like an app).

Your data stays in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose to use an AI feature or share a report.

## MaxedHealth Premium

Premium runs on your own Android phone and adds automatic wearable sync through Health Connect, device ranking, daily self-checks, automatic backups and updates, with your data kept on your phone, and personal setup help. [Register your interest](https://tally.so/r/Dq2AQ5).

## Not medical advice

MaxedHealth is a tracking tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat anything. Starter packs are starting points to edit, not recommendations.
